Hotels, rooming houses, and other single-room-occupancy structures hand-extracted from the Feb 1933 Sanborn fire-insurance survey — a railroad division point on the Southern Pacific, chosen to compare against El Paso.

About this map

Points mark structures labeled on the printed Sanborn plates (Hotel, Rooming, Boarding, Apartments). Positions are placed by a single-landmark, scale-bar + compass-rose transform per sheet — block-level accuracy, not parcel-level. See pipeline/georef.py for the method.
