About

Austin in 1900 was Texas's capital and a university town of about 22,000, with a downtown hotel district along Congress Avenue and San Jacinto/Brazos streets near the Capitol, boarding houses scattered through the near west and near east side neighborhoods, and institutional dormitories at the state's new deaf school, Tillotson College, St. Edward's College, and the University of Texas. Same manual, tile-by-tile vision-reading method used for El Paso, Alpine, and San Angelo.

Source

Sanborn Fire Insurance Map of Austin, Tex., Sanborn Map Co., 1900, 53 sheets (bound volume, Library of Congress call no. sanborn08415_004). Buildings recorded here are those explicitly labeled Hotel, Boarding, Rooms, Lodging, or Dormitories on the original sheets -- a lower bound, not a full census.
