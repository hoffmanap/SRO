Controls

blue dot — drag to move the sheet
red dot — drag to rotate
green dot — drag to resize (width & height stretch independently, if the sheet doesn't fit at its original aspect ratio)

Export

Your progress is saved in this browser as you go (it'll still be here if you close the tab). When you're done, download both files and send them back in chat.

Sheets

Saved sheets are listed in stacking order (top of list = bottom of stack). Drag a saved sheet onto another to reorder where sheets overlap.

About

Pick a sheet, drag it into place over the aerial photo (it should line up with the real street grid and block shapes), then hit Save. Switch basemaps with the layer control top-right. If a sheet's black border actually covers several separate, non-touching areas (common on index/key sheets with distant insets), use "Split sheet into pieces" to carve out each area and place them independently — each piece then gets its own row, handles, and stacking order, and exports as its own small image file alongside the manifest. Use Box mode for a straight-edged area, or Polygon mode when an area is angled or irregular and doesn't fit a rectangle.

Split into pieces

Box mode: drag a rectangle around one self-contained area. Polygon mode: click to place each corner, then click back on the first point (or double-click) to close the shape -- use this when an area doesn't fit a straight rectangle. Then click "Add piece." Repeat for each separate area. Existing pieces are outlined in orange and labeled.
